# MONTHLY_AUDITS.md — Deeper Monthly Audit

> Honest assessment of whether a real edge exists and whether active trading is
> justified at all.

Questions each month:
- Is there evidence of a genuine edge (vs luck)?
- Are returns improving? Is drawdown acceptable? Is risk controlled?
- Any sign of overfitting? Are transaction costs material?
- Strategy–regime mismatches? Trading too often? Rejecting too much?
- Are mistakes decreasing? Are live results materially worse than paper?
- **Would passive investing (e.g. SPY buy-and-hold) have done better?**
- Should capital allocation stay, decrease, or go to owner review?

```

Live vs paper divergence:
Verdict: continue as-is / reduce / escalate to owner
```
