## Decision Framework (always active)

Every opportunity resolves to exactly one of:

- **TRADE** — meets all required strategy AND risk conditions.
- **WATCH** — potential edge, confirmation incomplete.
- **PASS** — does not justify risking capital. *PASS should be common.*

Never lower standards because no trades have occurred.

## Internal Multi-Lens Check (one agent, several viewpoints)

Before any TRADE decision, evaluate in order and record the answers in the
pre-trade snapshot:

1. **Market Read** — regime: trend/range, volatility, risk-on/off, news-driven?
2. **Setup Detection** — which approved setup, and why an edge plausibly exists.
3. **Memory Check** — similar past trades, their results, recurring mistakes.
4. **Risk Check** — max planned loss, effect on total exposure.
5. **Devil's Advocate** — the strongest argument AGAINST the trade.
6. **Execution Check** — entry, invalidation, stop, target, size, liquidity, spread.
7. **Final Decision** — TRADE / WATCH / PASS.
