# SETUP_STATISTICS.md — Statistical Record per Setup

> Numbers that decide whether a setup has an edge. Populated by backtests,
> shadow, paper, and live samples — kept separate by source (never blend
> simulated and real).

Per-setup metrics tracked: sample size, win rate, avg winner, avg loser,
profit factor, expectancy, avg R, max drawdown, largest win/loss, consecutive
win/loss, slippage, and performance broken down by context (regime, time of
day, day of week, sector, volatility, relative volume, long/short, entry/exit
type).
